Expected CSV Format Guidelines

Your CSV file should have a header row with column titles matching or resembling the table below. Unrecognized categories will be automatically created under your account.

Date Category Amount Notes Payment Method
2026-10-01 Dining 24.50 Starbucks Coffee Credit Card
2026-10-02 Groceries 85.20 Supermarket run UPI / Card
2026-10-03 Transport 15.00 Metro pass recharge Cash
Dates can be formatted as YYYY-MM-DD or MM/DD/YYYY or DD-MM-YYYY.
Currency symbols ($, ₹, €, £) and commas inside amounts are automatically stripped.
Rows with negative amounts, zeros, or empty dates are safely skipped without failing the entire import.
